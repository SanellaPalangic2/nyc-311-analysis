# Top findings

Generated by `python src/findings.py`. Every number comes from the SQL files in `sql/analysis/` (file noted per finding); rerun the script to reproduce them.

Scope: 3,655,042 NYC 311 requests created in 2025; 3,599,434 have a valid resolution time. Overall median 6.61 h, 90th percentile 409.46 h, mean 237.29 h.

## 1. The agency handling a request matters far more than borough or season

- Range of medians across the 15 agencies: 1.26 h (NYPD) to 7,845.19 h (EDC). Across boroughs: 4.03 h (Queens) to 15.18 h (Staten Island). Across seasons: 4.01 h (Summer) to 14.00 h (Winter).
- NYPD handles 1,717,594 requests (46.99% of all) with a median of 1.26 h. The next-largest agency, HPD (774,535 requests), has a median of 79.19 h.
- The slowest agency, EDC, has 47.06% of requests with no close date, so even its median understates the delay.
- Source: `00_overview.sql`, `02_by_agency.sql`, `03_by_borough.sql`, `04_by_season.sql`

## 2. The season pattern reverses once NYPD is separated out (mix effect)

- All requests: Winter is slowest (14.00 h median) and Summer fastest (4.01 h).
- All agencies except NYPD: Summer is slowest (73.38 h) and Winter fastest (56.55 h).
- Why: two things lift Winter's overall median. NYPD, the fastest agency, is a smaller share of requests (42.77% in Winter vs 48.82% in Summer), and NYPD itself is slower in Winter (1.68 h vs 1.05 h). Meanwhile, for all other agencies combined, Winter is the fastest season.
- Source: `04_by_season.sql`, `07_season_mix.sql`

## 3. Borough differences depend on the complaint type

- Bronx vs citywide median for the same type: Illegal Parking 1.52x, Noise - Residential 1.48x, Abandoned Vehicle 1.44x (slower), but HEAT/HOT WATER 0.85x (faster).
- Manhattan: Illegal Parking 0.68x (faster) but UNSANITARY CONDITION 1.52x (slower).
- So no borough is simply slow or fast; it depends on which agency and problem.
- Source: `06_complaint_type_by_borough.sql`

## Caveats

- **Open requests:** 1.50% of all requests have no close date, but it is concentrated: e.g. Noise - Helicopter 47.06% (median 7,845.19 h, 0.01% closed within 30 days). Medians for such types are too optimistic; % closed within 30 days counts open requests and is the safer comparison. (`01`, `05`)
- **Possible automatic closures:** 4 complaint types (Non-Residential Heat, Mobile Food Vendor, Food Establishment, Smoking or Vaping) have medians between 1,440 and 1,441 hours (60 days), with 90th percentiles of 1,441.43-1,448.95 h. That pattern suggests a fixed 60-day closing rule rather than real work time; the data can't confirm the process. (`05`)
- **Not causal:** these are descriptive comparisons; complaint mix, staffing and how each agency closes requests are not separated out except where noted.
